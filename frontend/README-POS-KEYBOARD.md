# ApexStay POS keyboard workflow

The POS now supports keyboard-first product entry.

1. Open Point of Sale. The product command field is focused automatically.
2. Type an exact product name, SKU, or barcode and press **Enter** to add one unit. Barcode scanners that type the barcode into this field and send Enter work the same way.
3. To choose a quantity, type the exact product name, SKU, or barcode, press **\***, type the whole-number quantity, then press **Enter**.
4. Press **Esc** to leave quantity mode without adding the item.
5. Press **F2** to return focus to the product field, **F5** to checkout, and **F6** to enter a customer name.

The POS checks available stock before adding the requested quantity. Product tiles and cart buttons remain available as mouse alternatives.
